DMCA Takedown Request

Last updated: July 15, 2026

PLEXILE ARCADE respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects our users, partners, and content providers to do the same. If you believe that content available on our website infringes your copyright, you may submit a DMCA takedown request.

1. Copyright Infringement Notice

If you are a copyright owner or an authorized representative and believe that material available on PLEXILE ARCADE violates your copyright, please provide a written notice containing the following information:

  • Your full name and contact information.
  • A description of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed.
  • The URL or location of the material you believe is infringing.
  • A statement explaining why you believe the content violates your copyright.
  • A statement confirming that the information provided is accurate.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

2. How To Submit A Request

DMCA takedown requests should be sent through our official contact methods. Please include all required information so we can properly review your request.

Incomplete or inaccurate requests may delay the review process.

3. Review Process

After receiving a valid DMCA request, PLEXILE ARCADE will review the submitted information and determine the appropriate action.

Possible actions may include:

  • Removing or restricting access to the reported content.
  • Contacting relevant parties for additional information.
  • Taking other appropriate actions based on the circumstances.

4. False Claims

Submitting false or misleading copyright claims may result in legal consequences. Please ensure that all information included in your request is accurate before submitting it.

5. Third-Party Content

PLEXILE ARCADE may provide access to games, links, or content created by third parties. We do not claim ownership of third-party materials and respect the rights of their respective owners.

If you believe third-party content hosted or linked through our website infringes your rights, please contact us with the necessary details.

6. Counter Notification

If content has been removed due to a DMCA request and you believe the removal was incorrect, you may submit a counter notification with relevant information explaining your claim.

7. Contact Us

For copyright concerns, DMCA requests, or other intellectual property questions, please contact the PLEXILE ARCADE team through our official contact methods.

Thank you for helping us maintain a safe and respectful gaming community.
